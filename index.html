CUSTOM TILE WORK & REMODELING SERVICES

We combine professional interior design with precision tile installation and residential remodeling across the Greater Portland Metro Area and Yamhill County.

01 • Core Specialty

Custom Tile Work & Walk-In Showers

Expert tile layout, waterproofing, and installation for custom walk-in tile showers, vertical & subway tile tub surrounds, recessed shower niches, mosaic shower pans, large-format porcelain bathroom floors, and kitchen tile backsplashes across Greater Portland.

02 • Design + Build

Bathroom & Kitchen Remodeling

Complete bathroom and kitchen renovations in Portland and Newberg—including freestanding soaking tubs, frameless glass shower enclosures, double vanities, quartz & granite countertops, undermount sinks, and interior design planning.

03 • Flooring Contractor

Tile, Hardwood, LVP & LVT Flooring

Professional installation and refinishing of solid hardwood flooring, waterproof Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP), Luxury Vinyl Tile (LVT), custom ceramic and porcelain tile floors, and crisp baseboard trim.

04 • Painting & Refinishing

Interior, Exterior & Cabinet Painting

Transform your home with designer interior wall and trim painting, weather-protective Oregon exterior siding painting, and full kitchen cabinet refinishing.

05 • Millwork & Decks

Finish Trim, Porches & Cedar Decks

Custom window and door casings, baseboards, coffered ceilings, stair handrailings, and durable weather-resistant staining for cedar decks, covered wood porches, and privacy fences.

06 • Repairs & Custom Work

Drywall Repair & Custom Concepts

From water-damage drywall, subfloor, and tile restoration to custom architectural features, we work closely with your budget to solve complex remodeling challenges.

Common Questions

PORTLAND TILE & REMODELING FAQ

Answers to common questions about our custom tile installation, bathroom remodeling, and service areas across Greater Portland.

Do you specialize in custom tile work and walk-in showers?

Yes. Custom tile installation is our flagship specialty across the Greater Portland area. We build waterproof walk-in tile showers, vertical and subway tile tub surrounds, recessed shower niches, mosaic shower floors, heated tile floors, and kitchen backsplashes.

What cities around Portland does Raven Remodeling serve?

Established in 2024 and based in Newberg, OR, we serve the entire Greater Portland Metro Area including Portland, Beaverton, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Tualatin, Sherwood, Hillsboro, West Linn, Oregon City, Newberg, Dundee, and McMinnville.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured in Oregon?

Yes. Raven Remodeling LLC is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the State of Oregon for residential and small commercial remodeling projects.

Can you help with interior design as well as the remodeling work?

Yes. Co-founder Rayen is an Interior Designer who helps you plan layouts and choose tile, colors, and finishes that fit your budget, while co-founder Nicolas handles the tile installation, carpentry, flooring, and remodeling construction.
