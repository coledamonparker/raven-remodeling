TILE WORK & REMODELING SERVICES

We combine thoughtful interior design with skilled craftsmanship across the Greater Portland Metro Area and Yamhill County.

01 • Core Specialty

Custom Tile Work & Showers

Precision tile layout, waterproofing, and installation for walk-in showers, vertical & subway tile surrounds, recessed niches, mosaic shower pans, bathroom floors, and kitchen backsplashes.

02 • Design + Build

Bathroom & Kitchen Remodeling

Complete bathroom and kitchen renovations including freestanding soaking tubs, glass shower enclosures, double vanities, countertops, undermount sinks, and layout design.

03 • Flooring

Tile, Hardwood, LVP & LVT

Installation and refinishing of solid hardwood flooring, waterproof Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP), Luxury Vinyl Tile (LVT), custom tile floors, and clean baseboard trim.

04 • Painting

Interior, Exterior & Cabinets

Personalize your home with interior wall and trim painting, durable exterior siding paint, and full kitchen cabinet refinishing.

05 • Millwork & Decks

Trim, Porches & Cedar Decks

Custom window casings, baseboards, coffered ceilings, handrailings, and weather-protective staining for wood decks, porches, and fences.

06 • Repairs & Custom Work

Drywall & Custom Concepts

From water-damage drywall and flooring restoration to custom architectural concepts, we work closely with your budget to solve remodeling challenges.

About Us

NOT JUST A BUSINESS — BUT IT IS A BUSINESS.

Who We Are

Our passion lies in design, hard work, and connection. We take pride in continuously learning and developing new skills, while respecting those who achieve remarkable results through dedication and effort. Our aspiration is that our best project is always the next one.

Family-Owned in Oregon Since 2024

Raven Remodeling is a family-owned company based in Newberg, Oregon, serving the Greater Portland area and Yamhill County since 2024. Rayen is an Interior Designer and devoted mother of three, while Nicolas has devoted his life to residential and small commercial remodeling—specializing in custom tile work and full home renovations. Together, we are here to answer one essential question: “How can we help?”
